# SpotifyServer — brand guide

**Proposed display name:** SpotifyServer
**Tagline:** *The console's streaming backend.*

## Why this name

Kept — a backend component should be findable by what it wraps. The mark stays deliberately un-Spotify: an album tile, not arcs in a circle.

## The mark

An album tile with a play wedge and a progress bar mid-track.

## Palette

| Color | Hex | Role |
|---|---|---|
| Deep Green | `#1E5E3E` | Background |
| Sleeve Cream | `#FFF6EC` | Tile |
| Soft Green | `#A7D7BD` | Progress track |

> **Trademark note:** Spotify's name and circular-arcs logo are trademarks — this mark avoids the logo; keep the wrapper described as unofficial.
